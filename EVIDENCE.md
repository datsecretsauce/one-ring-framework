# Evidence Model

## REVIEWED
Static inspection only.

## TESTED
A defined check was executed.

## VERIFIED
Evidence supports the material claim.

## UNRESOLVED
Evidence cannot settle the claim.

## IMPLEMENTED BUT NOT VERIFIED
The change exists, but required runtime/integration evidence was not obtained.

## Consensus Fallacy Rule
Model consensus cannot close runtime-required claims. Runtime evidence or unresolved; no third state.
