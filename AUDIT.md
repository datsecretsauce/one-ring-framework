# Audit Contract

Minimum package:
- original brief and success criteria;
- tier and rationale;
- constraints/out-of-scope;
- previous version and/or diff;
- implementation;
- verification evidence;
- approved Stop Rule decisions.

Provenance should be withheld where practical, never falsified.

Every audit must produce findings plus the mandatory Coverage Statement. A no-findings audit means only **no findings in checked scope**, never universal correctness.
