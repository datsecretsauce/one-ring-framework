# Classification Ratchet

Assess **impact x reversibility x uncertainty**.

## PATCH
Low impact, readily reversible, low uncertainty.

## CHANGE
Meaningful impact OR meaningful reversal cost OR material uncertainty.

## COMMITMENT
High impact, difficult/costly to reverse, or material security/privacy/financial/public/contractual/architectural consequence.

## Ratchet
Builder: may escalate, may not downgrade.
Human: may downgrade only with recorded rationale.
Audit remediation: always re-enters at CLASSIFY.
If uncertain between tiers: choose the higher tier.
